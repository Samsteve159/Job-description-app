Lead Treasury Analyst - Product Owner - AI
Wells Fargo
Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. On-site. Full-time.

About This Role
Wells Fargo is seeking a Lead Treasury Analyst.

In This Role, You Will
Act as Lead Consultant to finance, accounting and Business Line management on matters related to corporate treasury and related financial management
Monitor and evaluate global market conditions, funding requirements, asset and liability management, liquidity risk management, capital management, financial performance management, capital, and related activities
Provide overall advisory on treasury finance and risk strategies
Ensure data quality and establish controlled processes
Lead targeted projects including guiding other teammates
Collaborate with Internal Audit, Corporate Risk Management and Model Governance teams as well as outside parties including regulatory agencies and accounting and consulting firms
Make recommendations to more experienced group members and management
Collaborate and consult with peers, colleagues and managers to resolve issues and achieve goals
Interact with internal customers
Receive direction from leaders and exercise independent judgment while developing the knowledge to understand function, policies, procedures, and compliance requirements

Required Qualifications:
5+ years of Treasury experience, or equivalent demonstrated through one or a combination of the following: work experience, training, military experience, education

Desired Qualifications:
Experience operating as a Product Owner or business lead for data, analytics, reporting, workflow, or technology-enabled delivery initiatives.
Ability to translate complex Treasury business needs into clear requirements, user stories, acceptance criteria, and delivery priorities.
Working familiarity with AI-enabled productivity, automation, or analytics concepts, with the ability to evaluate relevant business use cases and partner with technology teams on implementation.
Strong understanding of financial institution balance sheet fundamentals, product offerings, and balance sheet management practices.
Experience supporting one or more balance sheet products, such as capital, liquidity, funding, or interest rate risk.
5+ years of experience using SQL for data transformation, analytics, and large relational databases.
Experience designing technical or data solutions to address complex business problems.
Demonstrated ability to build strong partnerships and influence outcomes in a highly matrixed environment.
Experience with data analysis tools and platforms, such as Dremio, Python, S3 data storage, SAS, Power BI, or similar analytics environments.
Exposure to AI, machine learning, intelligent automation, agentic AI, natural language interfaces, or AI assisted testing and change management capabilities.
Ability to define controls, validation approach, and adoption measures for AI-enabled solutions used in regulated financial services processes.
Undergraduate degree in information systems, computer science, finance, or a related field.

Job Expectations:
Product Ownership and Agile Delivery: Serve as Product Owner for assigned Treasury data and analytics initiatives, leading agile delivery by defining requirements, prioritizing work, and guiding technology teams through established agile practices.
Solution Design and Delivery: Design and influence technical solutions to address complex Treasury business problems, partnering with engineering, architecture, and data teams to deliver scalable and well-controlled outcomes.
Backlog Management: Own and maintain a prioritized product backlog aligned to strategic Treasury initiatives, ensuring clear user stories, acceptance criteria, and delivery readiness.
Stakeholder Management: Build and maintain strong partnerships with Treasury stakeholders, data providers, and technology teams, managing expectations and dependencies to drive alignment and execution.
Data Analysis and Validation: Lead data analysis, testing, and validation for Treasury processes and regulatory reporting inputs, ensuring accuracy, control adherence, and audit readiness.
Communication and Influence: Translate complex technical and analytical concepts into clear, actionable insights for business and leadership stakeholders; provide regular progress updates and proactively escalate risks and issues.
Independent Judgment: Operate independently within established governance frameworks, applying sound judgment to balance business priorities, risk considerations, and policy requirements.
SME Leadership and Knowledge Sharing: Act as a subject-matter expert for assigned Treasury products and data domains, providing guidance, onboarding support, and knowledge sharing to junior product owners and analysts.

AI, Automation, and Digital Enablement Requirements
AI use case identification: Identify practical opportunities to apply AI, machine learning, agentic workflows, or automation within Treasury reporting, data validation, UAT, change management, and analytics processes.
AI-enabled product ownership: Translate AI or automation opportunities into business requirements, user stories, acceptance criteria, success measures, and implementation roadmaps that technology teams can execute.
Prompt and workflow design: Demonstrate working knowledge of prompt engineering, AI-assisted workflow design, and human-in-the-loop review patterns for business adoption.
Data readiness and controls: Partner with data, technology, and risk stakeholders to ensure AI use cases have clearly defined inputs, outputs, lineage, validation checks, access controls, and audit evidence.
Responsible AI mindset: Apply sound judgment around accuracy, explainability, privacy, data protection, model risk, and business controls when evaluating or implementing AI-enabled solutions.
AI adoption and enablement: Support training, documentation, adoption playbooks, and stakeholder communications to help Treasury users understand how to use AI-enabled capabilities safely and effectively.
Measurement and continuous improvement: Define measurable outcomes for AI-enabled initiatives, including productivity improvement, cycle-time reduction, defect reduction, user adoption, and control effectiveness.
