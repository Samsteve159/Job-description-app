Manager, Procurement Analytics
Mumbai, India. Hybrid. Global capability centre of a multinational.

About the role
You will own spend analytics end to end for a global procurement organisation, from raw
ERP and invoice data through to the savings case the business acts on.

Responsibilities
Own spend analysis across categories, from data ingestion to the recommendation
Build and maintain the reporting layer in Power BI, on models you design yourself
Audit supplier categorisation against UNSPSC taxonomy and keep it current
Run supplier master data management and deduplication
Identify savings opportunities and build the commercial case for each
Reconcile published figures to source systems so numbers survive challenge
Partner with finance on the financial impact of procurement decisions

Required
6+ years in spend analytics or procurement analytics
Advanced SQL for data transformation against large relational databases
Power BI or Tableau to a build standard, not just consumption
Hands-on experience with UNSPSC taxonomy and supplier master data
Demonstrated data quality and reconciliation discipline

Nice to have
Python for data pipelines
Machine learning applied to classification of spend data
FP&A exposure and comfort presenting to finance leadership
Experience with automation of manual reporting processes
